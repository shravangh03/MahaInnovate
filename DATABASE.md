# Database Schema & Entity Relationship Overview

The platform uses a Supabase PostgreSQL database schema with 17 relational tables:

1. `profiles`: User accounts and role definitions (`Government Officer`, `Startup`, `Evaluator`, `Admin`).
2. `departments`: Public sector ministry & department directory.
3. `challenges`: Government innovation problem statements and required tech specs.
4. `challenge_requirements`: Specific eligibility criteria weights per challenge.
5. `startups`: DPIIT startup profiles, founded year, team size, verification badges.
6. `startup_technologies`: Technology tag mappings for startups.
7. `proposals`: Submissions from startups against government challenges.
8. `evaluations`: Evaluator overall score and commentary records.
9. `evaluation_scores`: Detailed 7-criteria score breakdown per evaluation.
10. `pilots`: Active/completed sandbox trial deployments.
11. `pilot_kpis`: Target KPI definitions for pilots.
12. `kpi_measurements`: Actual telemetry readings logged during pilot trials.
13. `validations`: Independent audit verification certificates (PASSED / FAILED).
14. `procurements`: Final statewide procurement rollout allocations.
15. `reports`: Executive summary text reports generated by AI.
16. `notifications`: Real-time user notifications.
17. `activity_logs`: Audit logs tracking platform actions.
